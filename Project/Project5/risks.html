Potential Risks/Benefits

Some benefits to think about before boxing:

Physical Health Benefits Mental Health Benefits
Great source of cardio Good outlet
Trains muscles in whole body Learns discipline
Improves defensive capabilities Better self control and understanding dangers of fighting
Builds hand speed and power Great community and opportunity to meet others

While boxing has a lot of benefits some things to consider are how you would like to train. Many people who decide to pick the sport up when a lot older like 35+ should train purely for cardio and the benefits listed above and not professionally meaning no sparring and amateur bouts.

Actually, fighting and getting hit takes a tole on your body that once you reach a certain age isn't ideal to be dealing with. Fighting is a young man's sport but training is for everyone.

If you are someone on the younger side ideally under 25 then you are young enough to really consider fighting amaturely and possibly working towards professional fighting.

If you do decide this is the path, you want to take here are some ways to reduce risk of possible long term health problems:

  • Find a gym that has a good culture and coaches that prioritize the STUDENTS health (that means wearing head gear when sparring and not allowing newer people to just jump into hard sparring with zero technique)
  • Taking breaks and not pushing through recurring head pain
  • Communicating with coaches about injuries and how you feel daily
  • Don't take unnecessary hard hits to the head to "build up toughness" — that isn't real
  • Work on punching technique and defense (perfect the basics)
